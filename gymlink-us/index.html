Why it actually works

Plans built for your floor, not for a stock photo.

Generic workout apps prescribe exercises your club may not own. GymLink starts from your equipment inventory, club by club, and generates plans that can only contain movements a member can actually perform when they walk in.

  • Inventory once, plans forever. Each location registers what it has. Plans and substitutions are constrained to that list automatically.
  • Train at any of your clubs. A member who switches location gets the same program, re-mapped to that building's equipment. Multi-site membership stops being a downgrade.
  • Busy machine, instant swap. When the rack is occupied, the app offers an equivalent that is free — instead of the member standing around, then leaving.
  • Purchasing backed by evidence. The same data tells you which machines are queued and which have been idle for months, before the next capex cycle.

Questions operators ask

The awkward ones, answered.

Does GymLink replace my gym management software?

No. GymLink sits on top of your billing and access control. Your member records, contracts, and payments stay where they are. GymLink owns the training experience: plans, trainer workflow, and floor analytics. That means no billing migration and no risk to your revenue during rollout.

What does "equipment-aware programming" actually mean?

Every location uploads its equipment inventory once. Training plans are then generated against what that club actually has on the floor, and substitutions are proposed automatically when a machine is busy or out of service. A member who trains at three of your clubs gets a plan that works at all three.

How long does implementation take?

The standard path is a two-week analysis, a 30-day pilot in one club, then a rollout of roughly one club per week. Nothing is signed for the full estate before the pilot has produced numbers you can check.

Is GymLink SOC 2 certified?

A SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress for the US launch. Data for US customers is stored in US regions, member data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the platform supports CCPA and CPRA data subject requests. We state the current status plainly in every RFP rather than implying a certification we do not yet hold.

What happens to my trainers' jobs?

Nothing good happens to a gym that automates its trainers away. GymLink is built to make the trainer the most visible person in the building: it hands them a prioritized list, removes the paperwork, and makes their expertise reach members who would otherwise never speak to them. Operators running it see personal training bookings go up, not down.

Do you have US customers yet?

Not yet — the launch partner program is how we get our first ones, and we would rather say that than show logos we have not earned. What we do have is a product that has been live in the Swiss market and two years of operational research with chains that run dozens of clubs.

How is GymLink priced?

Per location, per month, with unlimited members and unlimited trainer seats included. Launch pricing starts at $349 per location per month billed annually. Multi-location chains are quoted per estate. There is no per-member fee, so retention work is never taxed.
